**Prompt** futuristic town flooded with aliens as civilians. using sharp and soft lines to outline mood and tone. Use mostly squares and rectangles. use soft texture and a lot of volume to show how huge the city is. using bright overwheling lights.
**Glossary** Lines are markers that can create shapes or definite/outline something. Shape is the form of something that outlines something. Contrast causes the opposite of whatever is the subject/object could be light to dark, small to big, etc...
